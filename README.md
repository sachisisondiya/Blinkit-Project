# Blinkit-Project
Project Overview – BlinkIT Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

The BlinkIT Sales Dashboard is designed to analyze grocery sales performance, customer ratings, product categories, and outlet performance. It helps stakeholders monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, and make data-driven decisions to improve revenue and operational efficiency.

Key Dashboard Features
Sales performance tracking across different outlet types and locations.
Product category analysis by Item Type.
Outlet performance comparison based on size and establishment year.
Customer rating analysis.
Item Fat Content analysis (Low Fat vs Regular).
Interactive slicers for filtering data by:
Outlet Size
Outlet Location Type
Item Type
Trend analysis using line charts and funnel visualizations.
Key Metrics (KPIs)
Total Sales
Average Sales
Average Rating
Total Items Sold
Sales by Outlet Type
Sales by Outlet Size
Sales by Item Type
Sales by Fat Content
Sales by Outlet Location
Outlet-wise Performance Trend
Key Insights Included
Best-performing outlet types contribute the highest revenue.
Identification of top-selling product categories.
Comparison of sales between Low Fat and Regular products.
Customer satisfaction measurement through average ratings.
Analysis of how outlet size and location impact sales.
Sales trends across outlet establishment years.
Key Outcomes
Improved visibility into overall business performance.
Faster identification of high-performing and low-performing outlets.
Better inventory and product category planning.
Enhanced decision-making through real-time KPI monitoring.
Increased focus on customer preferences and satisfaction.
Support for strategic expansion and sales growth initiatives.

Project Objective:
To provide a comprehensive and interactive sales analytics solution that enables BlinkIT management to monitor performance, optimize operations, and drive business growth through actionable insights.
